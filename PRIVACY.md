# Privacy

Shopper MCP runs entirely on your computer.

- **No data collection.** No analytics, telemetry or accounts. The author receives nothing.
- **Your session stays local.** Login, cookies and delivery address live in a browser profile on your machine and never leave it.
- **Marketplace only.** The server talks only to the marketplace sites you use (now ozon.ru), the same way your browser does. Their own privacy policies apply.
- **Live tab link** works on `127.0.0.1` only and expires after 15 minutes.

Delete the profile folder to remove everything.
